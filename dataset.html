The Dataset

The Religious Leader Tracker is built on a hand-researched dataset covering current leadership across 150+ religious denominations worldwide: who leads each body, when their tenure began, and how many members the body claims. It is maintained continuously as leadership changes occur.

leaders.csv Primary leadership table — source of truth membership-data.json Membership figures with sources leaders-research-log.csv Research audit trail for tenure dates

Schema — leaders.csv

ColumnDescription
religionTop-level religion (Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, Judaism, …).
DenominationMajor branch or family within the religion (e.g. Catholic, Sunni, Mahāyāna).
sub denominationIntermediate grouping where one exists (e.g. Eastern Orthodoxy, Pure Land).
sub denomination 2The specific body being tracked (e.g. Russian Orthodox Church, Soka Gakkai).
membersClaimed membership where known; n/a otherwise. Figures also live in membership-data.json with sources.
sourceSource for the membership figure.
leaderCurrent leader, usually as Name / Title.
sinceTenure start date. Day precision (e.g. May 8th 2025) where research allows; month or year precision otherwise.
daysUnused in the data — days in office are computed live on the site.

Methodology

Leadership entries are researched primarily from Wikipedia infoboxes and official denominational sources, then refined for date precision. Every tenure-date decision is recorded in leaders-research-log.csv, including the field used (e.g. term_start), the source type, the source URL, and matching notes — so any figure on the site can be traced back to where it came from.

  • Membership figures are as claimed by the denomination or as estimated by the cited source; they are not independently verified.
  • Day counts are only computed for tenures with day-precision start dates.
  • Recent changes are published in the Succession Log and its RSS feed.
