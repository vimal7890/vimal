Intro

The Bulgarian Greek Catholic Church is an Eastern Catholic church in Bulgaria that celebrates the Byzantine Rite in Bulgarian and is organized as a single eparchy.

Structure

The Bulgarian Greek Catholic Church consists of the Bulgarian Catholic Eparchy of Saint John XXIII of Sofia.
